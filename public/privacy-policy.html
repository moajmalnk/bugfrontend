Google User Data

BugRicer offers optional Google integrations. We only access Google user data after you choose to sign in with Google or connect your Google account, and only for the features below.

  • Sign in with Google (openid, email, profile): Your Google account ID, email address, name, and profile picture are used to create or sign in to your BugRicer account.
  • Google Docs (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents): Create and edit BugDocs documents (bug reports, meeting notes, templates) that you start from BugRicer.
  • Google Sheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets): Create and edit BugSheets spreadsheets (bug tracking sheets) that you start from BugRicer.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Access only the files BugRicer creates or that you open with BugRicer. We cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar): Create calendar events with Google Meet links when you schedule a BugMeet meeting.

How we store it: We store an OAuth refresh token (encrypted in transit, kept on our servers to keep the integration working), your Google account ID and Google email address. We store links (IDs and URLs) to the documents, spreadsheets and meetings you create through BugRicer. We do not copy or store the contents of your other Google files.

How we share it: Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties, except where required by law. It is not used to train AI or machine learning models. BugRicer staff do not read your Google data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security purposes, or it is required by law.

Disconnect and deletion: You can disconnect Google at any time from BugDocs, BugSheets or your Profile, which deletes the stored tokens immediately. You can also revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or email info@bugricer.com to delete all Google-related data. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

Limited Use: BugRicer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google OAuth tokens are deleted as soon as you disconnect Google or delete your account.
